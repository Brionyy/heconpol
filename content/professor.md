서울대학교 보건대학원 보건정책관리학과 교수

서울대학교 경영대학(1987년)과 보건대학원(1989년)을 졸업하고 미국 University of Pennsylvania의 Wharton School에서 박사학위를 취득함 (1993년). University of Southern California에서 조교수를 역임하고 1997년부터 서울대에 재직중이며 2012년부터 2014년까지 보건대학원 원장 역임. 2020년 서울대학교 교육상 수상.

Harvard 대학교, London School of Economics, 캐나다 Toronto 대학교, 독일 Bremen 대학교, 독일 Trier 대학교, 북경대학교, 동경대학교 등에서 visiting professor/fellow를 역임. World Bank, WHO, GIZ의 consultant 로 전세계 40 여개 국의 보건정책 자문. 2016-17년에는 학교를 휴직하고 ADB(Asian Development Bank)의 Chief of Health Sector Group 으로 근무. 2021-22년에는 학교를 휴직하고 한국보건산업진흥원 원장으로 재직. 현재 WHO Collaborating Centre for Health System and Financing 소장.

한국보건행정학회 회장, 보건경제학회 회장, 노년학회 회장, 보건대학원 협의회 회장을 역임함. 저명 국제학술지 Health Policy (Elsevier) 와 International Journal of Health Economics and Management (Springer)의 아시아 지역 editor 임. 건강보험정책심의위원회, 국제개발협력위원회, 저출산고령사회위원회, 사회보장심의위원회, 장기요양위원회 (부위원장, 산하 실무위원회 위원장) 등 주요 위원회의 위원 역임.

## Profile

Professor and Former Dean of the School of Public Health, Seoul National University (SNU), Republic of Korea

Prof Kwon is a health economist and has worked over 30 years on UHC, health finance, and health systems & policy. He has held visiting positions at the Harvard School of Public Health, London School of Economics, University of Toronto, University of Tokyo, Peking University, University of Trier, and University of Bremen. He was the Chief of the Health Sector Group in the Asian Development Bank (ADB) in 2016-2017. He is the founding director of the WHO Collaborating Centre for Health System and Financing at Seoul National University. He received the Excellence in Education award of Seoul National University in 2020. He was the President of the Korea Health Industry Development Institute (KHIDI) in 2021-22.

He has served as president of leading academic societies, including the Korean Society of Health Policy and Management, the Korean Health Economic Association, the Korean Association of Schools of Public Health, and the Korean Gerontological Society. He is the Asia Region Editor of Health Policy (Elsevier) and International Journal of Health Economics and Management (Springer), and was the editor of the Korean Journal of Public Health and the Korean Journal of Health Economics. He holds PhD from the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania (1993) and taught at the University of Southern California School of Public Policy until 1996.

He has been a member of board or advisory committees of Health Systems Global (HSG), WHO Alliance for Health Policy and Systems Research, GAVI (Global Alliance for Vaccine and Immunization), and WHO Centre for Health and Development. He has occasionally been a short-term consultant of WHO, World Bank, and GIZ for health system and financing in Algeria, Armenia, Barbados, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, Egypt, Ethiopia, Fiji, Ghana, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Lao PDR, Malaysia, Maldives, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Oman, Pakistan, Philippines, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Uzbekistan, and Vietnam.
